# Bundled fonts

The 34 `.woff2` files in this directory come from [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com)
and are redistributed here under their original open licences. Self-hosting them
is what makes both apps work offline and on any static host; nothing here
depends on a font CDN at runtime.

Re-download or update them with `tools/fetch-fonts.sh`, which pulls the latin
subset of each family from the Google Fonts CSS2 API.
