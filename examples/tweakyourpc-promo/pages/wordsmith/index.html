            How Wordsmith Studio works

            1. Compose. Type a source phrase and build an anagram of it. The letter ledger shows what is left or overused; suggestions only offer words you can still make.
            2. Refine. Choose Replace selected word to try alternatives for a word, or Finish it to find every way to use the remaining letters.
            3. Animate. Animate this sends the pair to the stage, where each letter travels to its new place. Try a look, then adjust anything in the inspector.
            4. Export. Make a GIF or video, encoded on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

            Shortcuts: Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z redo, Alt+1/2/3 switch workspace, Space play or pause in Animate, , and . step a frame.
