# Authors

**Christopher Davis** ([github.com/tweakyourpc](https://github.com/tweakyourpc)): idea author and
orchestrator. Conceived Showrig as a director's wizard over an offline, procedural film pipeline,
and directed its design from the tweakyourpc showreel.

Showrig drives these tools as separate programs and does not bundle them:
[Blender](https://www.blender.org) (GPL),
the ArtCraft suite's FilmCraft and EffectCraft (MIT or Apache-2.0),
[FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org) (LGPL/GPL),
[Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) (Apache-2.0),
[Playwright](https://playwright.dev) (Apache-2.0)
and optionally [Ollama](https://ollama.com) (MIT).
